# Scent Census identity (locked 2026-10-20)

This file is the tiebreaker. When a page, copy line, or feature fights back,
read this, then decide. Every claim here traces to PRD-v1, RESEARCH-naming,
or live code from the M0-M7 sprint.

## The one sentence

Scent Census is a public census of what actually smells close: real votes
from people who own both bottles, ranked with counted samples, on top of a
140,000-bottle catalogue you can track your whole shelf in.

## Who we are (and are not)

We are a census, a shelf tracker, and a creator rail. Ranked by votes, never
by ads. We are not a store, not an affiliate farm, not a fan page, not an
editorial "top 10" list. When money eventually enters (affiliate links), it
sits outside the ranking and is labeled; the census bucket data is never
for sale.

## The wedge (deliberate, from PRD section 2)

Clones are the door. Clone houses are where the whole young fragrance
internet lives, and every existing tool treats clones as an afterthought.
The census question, "which clone actually smells like the real thing?",
is only answerable with our data, so it leads the homepage, the metadata,
and the OG cards.

The door is wide behind it. The product serves the full collector journey
(PRD personas: the newbie who owns 2 to 10 bottles and fears a $200 blind
buy; the clone hunter; the collector). Homepage copy and the proof strip
already say: track designer, niche, and budget clones on one shelf.

## How money enters (docs/MONETIZATION.md)

The census is never for sale. A partner pays for a click, never for a
position. Three legs: retailer partnerships (prices and commission for
traffic), creator partnerships (a citable census page for their verdict),
and ad slots that are excluded from any page where the sponsor has a stake
in the fragrance. The ranking code reads no partner, no commission, and no
ad slot.

## Brand rules

- Brand name: Scent Census. Per RESEARCH-naming: fluent, spellable,
  distinct, "census" reads as measured, hence honest.
- The tagline "The Clone Census" names the census feature and its campaign,
  never the company. It appears as kicker, llms.txt framing, and
  JSON-LD alternateName so both names resolve to one entity.
- Domains: scentcensus.com + .app are available and on the purchase
  checklist (buy as a pair, redirect one).

## Voice and proof rules (inherited from the publishing rules)

- Plain, dry confidence. A census does not do hype. Short sentences land.
- Numbers on screen come from the database, server-rendered, never invented
  (counts, sample sizes, prices, votes). No claim of community scale until
  it exists; at zero votes we say "the first votes are yours to place".
- Rank honesty is the brand: 10 votes shows "low confidence", 25 drops the
  label, ties read as tied. Showing the machinery IS the identity.
- No em dashes, no AI tells, no rule-of-three constructions.

## What the identity means for what we build, in order

1. Census pages stay the indexable surface: every fragrance page is a
   permalink where a debate lives, with unique title and description.
2. Shareable shelf (public shelf URL with its own OG card): the artifact
   people post. This is the top missing viral loop.
3. /about page: how the census works, the bucket mechanics, the honesty
   floors. One page that doubles as SEO trust and AI grounding.
4. Census coverage growth: 34 clone pairs today; curation widens the graph.
5. Creator pickups feed the census publicly (PRD 7.7: claim-verified only).
6. Licensed bottle photography before launch traffic.

## Explicitly deferred (from the PRD, unchanged)

Storefronts, dropship catalogs, social feed plagiarism, scraped creator
content, invented prices, editorial scores.
