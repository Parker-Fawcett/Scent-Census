# Research: Imagination dupes vs what the census shows

Date: 22 Oct 2026
Scope: Louis Vuitton Imagination (corpus id 67370, slug `imagination-67370`).

## Short answer

Yes. There is a clear divide, and it is wider than for almost any other popular
original in the catalogue.

The dupe market names roughly 17 Imagination clones. The census displays 4 of
them. Our best cross-house number for the bottle is 70 percent. The content
sites publish "88 to 93 percent similar" for the same bottles. Those two numbers
are not measuring the same thing, and nothing on the page says so.

Three separate gaps stack up: coverage, threshold, and scale.

## Divide 1: coverage

Nine of the seventeen named dupes cannot appear on the page at all.

Five brands are absent from the Fragrantica corpus: Montagne Parfums, ALT
Fragrances, Scentedelic, EM5, and the Fragrance World release itself. Montagne's
Imaginary is the single most recommended Imagination clone on Reddit and it has
no corpus entry, so no vote can ever attach to it.

Four more exist in the catalogue with literally zero "reminds me of" votes
against Imagination: Dua #Imagine, Karus Blu Spice, Essence de Blanc, and
Alexandria Visionary. They are catalogued, they are correctly named as
Imagination inspirations by their own makers, and the census has nothing to say
about them.

This is the part a knowledgeable reader notices first. The page looks thin, not
wrong.

## Divide 2: threshold

Four dupes are measured and dropped anyway.

- Hawas Kobra misses by 0.3 points. It sits at 50.28 percent agree, and its
  Wilson lower bound is 47.7 percent, under our 50 percent rule. The community
  ranks it third. A coin flip is genuinely not enough evidence, so the exclusion
  is defensible, but the visible result is that the community's number three never
  reaches the page, for a reason no visitor can see.
- Hectic (36 percent), Full House (34 percent), and Art of Arabia I (38 percent)
  are nowhere near the floor. They are not close calls.

Scento calls Hectic "a pure DNA match" at "88 to 93 percent." Fragrantica voters
say 36 percent. That is a 50 point gap on the same bottle.

## Divide 3: scale

Imagination is the second most contested dupe target among popular originals in
the catalogue.

Method: take every original with at least 10 candidates that clear the vote floor
(25 total votes, 15 upvotes), compute the average agreement share across its
candidates, then rank.

- 1,927 originals qualify. Imagination ranks 132nd from the bottom, the bottom 7
  percent.
- Restrict to popular originals with 5,000 or more candidate votes. 220 qualify.
  Imagination ranks 2nd lowest. Only Perles de Lalique is lower.
- Imagination averages 45.8 percent agreement. Perles de Lalique is 44.9. The
  next three are Lazy Sunday Morning at 47.4, Alien Goddess at 47.5, Soir de
  Lune at 47.6.

For comparison, across the stored graph (20,425 edges, 15,612 of them
cross-house) the median agreement is 71.6 percent and the 90th percentile is
88.2 percent. Club de Nuit Intense Man to Aventus, the canonical "everyone
agrees this is a clone" pair, is 86.7 percent on 7,079 votes.

Of Imagination's 16 eligible candidates, exactly 2 clear 60 percent. One of them
is its own flanker. The other 10 sit between 33.6 and 38.3 percent, and they span
houses with nothing in common: Guerlain, Bujairami, Jo Milano, Maison Viegas,
Maison Alhambra, Assaf, Nuancielo. Shared chemistry does not produce a cluster
that tight across unrelated brands. A listing that nobody seconded does.

## The number problem

The market's "88 to 93 percent similar" figures have no published method.

The Scento ranking that circulates for this query lists Imagination at "320 to
370 EUR" in one table, "280 to 320 EUR" in another, "295 to 300 EUR" in a third,
and "305 to 370 USD" in a fourth, on the same page, checked the same month. It
calls ALT Reality "the closest on a like-for-like sniff" and then says Dua
#Imagine is "the most faithful reconstruction here." Its similarity scores are
asserted, not derived.

Our number is a vote share: of the people who voted on this pair, how many said
it reminds them of the original. That is a real measurement of a different
question, and it is systematically lower than a similarity score because it
counts dissent.

Which means the page currently makes an honest number look like a bad result.
Zara Sunrise at 57 percent reads as "mediocre dupe" to someone who has watched a
reviewer call it an exact match. Both statements are true and they are not
commensurable.

## What this means

The census is not wrong about Imagination. It is thin, and the thinness is
structural rather than accidental:

1. The most-praised clones are sold by online-only houses with no Fragrantica
   footprint.
2. The pairs that do exist carry unusually heavy dissent, because Imagination is
   a hyped fresh release that attracts comparison votes from people who tried one
   dupe and were disappointed.
3. Our floor correctly refuses to promote a coin flip, and the community's third
   favourite is a coin flip.

## Recommended fixes, ranked

1. **Say what the number means, at the point of the number.** One line above the
   census rows: "Close is the share of voters who said it reminds them of
   Imagination, not a similarity score." Low effort, removes the false
   expectation. The explainer already says this at the bottom of the page, which
   is after the reader has formed an opinion.

2. **Add an unmeasured shelf.** A short, curated block on the product page for
   named clones we cannot score yet, with the reason: "Sold by houses outside the
   catalogue" or "Catalogued, no votes yet." Imagination alone would need
   seventeen entries, and the same pattern repeats on other hyped fresh releases.
   It converts the biggest weakness into a visible act of honesty, and it is the
   only way Montagne and ALT ever appear on the page.

3. **Surface the contest, as a fact.** "Fewer than half the scents listed against
   Imagination clear 60 percent agreement. Second lowest of 220 popular originals
   in the catalogue." This is true, it is ours, and no competitor can print it.

4. **Capture prices for this cluster.** Zero of the seventeen have a price point.
   The value gap cannot render, so every one of these pages falls back to plain
   search links. Zara, Arabiyat, Al Haramain, and Rasasi all sell through
   discounters we already link.

## What would settle the cause

The current data cannot distinguish "hard to clone" from "attracts hostile
voters." Two things would:

- First-party side-by-side votes, where the voter has to confirm they have
  smelled both bottles before the vote counts. That is the creator token work and
  the shelf feature, already in progress.
- A look at the vote timeline on the Imagination page. If the dissent arrived in
  bursts after specific review videos, it is audience behaviour, not chemistry.

## What shipped from this research (2026-10-22)

Fixes 2 and 3 are live on the Imagination page. Fix 1 now has a delivery route.
Fix 4 is still open.

- `CloneClaim` table, `data/clones/louis-vuitton-imagination.json`, and
  `scripts/ingest-clone-claims.ts`. Sixteen claims, twelve with a published note
  match, five with a verified price. Schema and sourcing rules:
  `docs/DATA-clone-claims.md`.
- A "Named elsewhere" block on the product page. Each row states the strongest
  evidence available, the price with its size, who made the claim, and links to
  the source. Products in the catalogue link to their own page; products outside
  it get a ghost bottle.
- Three of the rows are market favourites (Bujairami Hectic, Jo Milano Full
  House, Maison Viegas Heaven). They carry real vote counts, 36, 34 and 34
  percent, because the corpus voted on them and the graph ingest dropped them
  for missing the floor. The claim stores those counts and the row says "below
  the census floor". Before this, the page was silent about three of the most
  recommended clones in the market.
- Note match is computed, never asserted: weighted harmonic mean of note-list
  recall and precision, floored so a product missing a note cannot display 100.
  Seven sellers publish Imagination's exact ten notes and score 100. French
  Avenue Essence de Blanc publishes a different pyramid and scores 34.
- The contested-original line renders above the block, using the 34 to 38
  percent cluster and the 16 eligible comparison lists.
- Fix 1 is partly addressed by a "Note match" row in "Where these numbers come
  from" and the gloss above the block. The census rows themselves still show a
  bare percentage.
- Fix 4 is not done. No price point exists for the Zara, Arabiyat, Al Haramain,
  or Rasasi cluster, so the value gap still cannot render there.
