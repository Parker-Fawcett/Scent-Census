# Imagination is the outlier, not the clones

The question: is Hectic (and the other market picks) genuinely bad at 36%, or is Imagination the problem?

## The test

For each of the 16 Imagination candidates, compute their average agreement
across ALL their other pairs (excluding Imagination). If a candidate scores
60-80% against other originals, it is a normal, crowd-pleasing scent. If it
also scores 60-80% against Imagination, then Imagination is cloneable and the
market is right. If it drops to 34-38% against Imagination specifically, then
Imagination is the outlier.

## Result

Every candidate scores 54-85% against other originals. None of them are hard
to clone in general. They all drop against Imagination.

## Imagination is the most contested original in the catalogue

Among 348 popular originals (5,000+ candidate votes), Imagination ranks #1
lowest average agreement at 45.8%. The next lowest is Shalimar Parfum Initial
at 47.9%. The median across all popular originals is 61.4%.

Imagination's 16 candidate pairs average 45.8%. The graph median is 71.6%.
Every candidate that scores 60-85% against other originals scores 33-57%
against Imagination. The chemistry of Imagination (citrus, black tea,
ambroxan, olibanum) apparently produces agreement rates that no other popular
original sees.

## The one exception

Zara Sunrise on the Red Sand Dunes Intense scores 69.9% against Imagination,
higher than its 57.8% average against other originals. It is the only clone that
works better on Imagination than on anything else. If there is a "correct" clone,
the voters say it is this one, not Hectic.

## What this means for the "hostile voters" hypothesis

The data rules out "the clones are bad." Marwa is a crowd-pleaser at 80.1%
against others. Hectic is fine at 60.1%. They are not defective products. They
just do not remind people of Imagination.

Two hypotheses remain:

1. **Hard to clone.** Imagination's accord (citrus + black tea + ambroxan +
   olibanum) is genuinely difficult to reproduce. The voters can tell the
   difference even when the note list is identical.

2. **Audience behavior.** Imagination owners are a specific, opinionated
   group. They vote "no" at higher rates than the general population, possibly
   because they paid $360 and want to protect the original's status. The vote
   timeline would settle this, but the corpus snapshot has no timestamps.

The first hypothesis is supported by the data: the drop is consistent across
all 16 candidates regardless of house, price, or note match. If it were audience
behavior, you would expect some candidates to escape the band. None do, except
Zara Intense.

## The market's "90% similar"

Hectic is the market's #1 pick. Its note match is 100% (identical list). Its
vote share against Imagination is 35.9%. The market's "90% similar" claim has
no published method, contradicts its own prices four times on the same page,
and calls two different products "closest" in different paragraphs.

The gap is not 54 points of hidden similarity. It is 54 points of marketing
assertion versus measured dissent.
